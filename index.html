How the score is built

  1. Liquidity stacking: on every capture, the rightmost strip of the heatmap (the most recent order-book snapshot) is turned into a per-row liquidity profile. The row median removes bubbles. Rows below the price line count as bids and rows above as asks. Closer levels weigh more, and bright "walls" add a bonus. More bids stacked below than asks above reads as bullish support. Select "Magnet" to invert this reading.
  2. Stacking change: whether the imbalance is moving toward bids or asks within the interval, meaning orders are being added or pulled.
  3. Bubble color bias: newly printed green vs red bubble pixels.
  4. Bubble drift: whether new prints at the live edge are landing higher or lower. This is the price-direction proxy.
  5. BUY / SELL: each interval (15 s, 1 m, 5 m) gets its own BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL label. When all three turn BULLISH for the confirm captures, the voice says Buy; when all three turn BEARISH, it says Sell. A cooldown stops repeat alerts on the same side when the 15 s label flickers.
  6. Score: the weighted average of the components, from −100 to +100. A label turns on above the enter threshold after the confirm captures, and holds until the score falls below the exit threshold.
