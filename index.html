Instructions

  1. Open the Bookmap YouTube video, switch to Theater mode and set quality to the highest available.
  2. Click Share YouTube/Bookmap tab and select the video tab.
  3. Click Auto-detect ES/NQ, then confirm the numbers and preview strips match the highlighted current prices.
  4. Click Start monitoring. If auto detection fails, choose Manual ladder selection and draw full narrow price-column strips for both markets.
  5. Download the CSV log to review missed detections, bad readings and labels.

Diagnostics

The auto detector uses on-screen OCR and heuristics to rank price labels. Color and highlight scoring are imperfect and could falsely track a static ladder number. Flat readings may be marked stale. This prototype cannot reliably measure livestream-to-exchange latency or reconstruct genuine Bookmap order flow.
