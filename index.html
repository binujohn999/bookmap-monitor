NQ Dual-Source Monitor V5

One market: NQ • Bookmap visual activity + TradingView on-screen price • two independently shared tabs/windows • no ES

Experimental visual monitor—not a live order-flow feed or a trading predictor. A YouTube Bookmap stream can be delayed compared with TradingView, so the two sources may describe different moments. Color-change counts do not measure genuine buy/sell execution volume. TradingView price is read from pixels using OCR and can be wrong, delayed, or for a different contract. Treat BULLISH/BEARISH as unvalidated observations, not trade signals.

1. Bookmap — NQ only

Share the YouTube/Bookmap tab or window. Drag a region around NQ heatmap trade bubbles, excluding price ladder, volume bars and ES.

Share your TradingView NQ chart separately. Draw around ONE readable current-price number—preferably a fixed-position quote rather than the moving right-axis label.

Manual prices are snapshots, not a continuous feed. Re-enter as prices move. OCR requires an internet download of Tesseract.js, then runs in your browser.

3. Monitor controls

Share both sources and select the NQ heatmap and TradingView price box.
Browser permissions: The two Share buttons each open their own Chrome/Edge screen-share picker. Choose the Bookmap tab/window first, then the TradingView tab/window. Do not choose this monitor tab. The final label requires sufficient motion, at least ~20 seconds of history, a fresh price, and agreement between the visual color-bias and price movement. Otherwise it displays NEUTRAL or WAIT. Even agreement is not evidence the delayed feeds are synchronized.

Setup checklist

  1. Open your Bookmap stream, enlarge the NQ chart and share its tab.
  2. Click NQ preset and adjust the blue area to contain only NQ heatmap bubbles.
  3. Open TradingView on the same NQ futures contract and share that tab or window separately.
  4. Draw the orange box around one stable live price display; click Test price OCR, visually verify it, then Confirm detected price.
  5. Click Start NQ monitoring. If OCR fails, manually enter a price as a temporary fallback.

Diagnostics

No uploaded video and no server processing. Captured frames stay in this browser. Tesseract OCR loads from a third-party CDN. Stop sharing independently at any time.
