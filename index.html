How to use

  1. Open your Bookmap YouTube livestream in another tab.
  2. Click Share Bookmap video tab, then choose the YouTube tab (or the Bookmap window, if available).
  3. Choose ES in the drawing selector and drag over its single current-price label. Repeat for NQ.
  4. Press Start price reading. Check both recognized prices against the stream before trusting any trend label.
  5. Use Download CSV to inspect or backtest the experimental readings.
Tip: Set YouTube playback quality to high resolution. Sharing a visible Bookmap desktop app window may give less delay than YouTube, but it still does not provide direct order-book data.
